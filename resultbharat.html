Eligibility Criteria

  • Touring Veterinary Officer and Equivalent: - 
  • Educational Qualification: Mandatory Degree in B.V.Sc. or B.V.Sc. & A.H. in Related Subject from a recognized University approved by the Veterinary Council of India (VCI).
  • Professional Registration: Must be permanently registered with the Bihar Veterinary Council.
  • For Complete Details, Read the Official Notification.

 

  • Fishery Extension Officer:- 
  • Educational Qualification: 4-Year Bachelor's Degree in Related Subject (Fisheries Science / B.F.Sc.) from a Fisheries College under an Agricultural University recognized by ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research), New Delhi
  • For Complete Details, Read the Official Notification.

BTSC Fishery Extension / Touring Veterinary Officer Recruitment 2026: Pay Scale & Mode of Selection

  • Pay Scale:  Touring Veterinary Officer and Equivalent:- Pay Matrix Level 9 (₹53,100 – ₹1,67,800/-)   ||  Fishery Extension Officer:- Pay Matrix Level 7

BTSC Fishery Extension / Touring Veterinary Officer Recruitment 2026: Document Required / Inportant Instruction

Candidates must ensure the following certificates and mark sheets are uploaded in the prescribed columns of the online application:

  • (i) Matriculation Certificate: Original certificate for proof of age/identity.

  • (ii) Educational Qualification:  Upload Certificate.

  • (iii) Mandatory Experience Certificate: Upload Certificate.

  • (iv) General Experience Certificate: For candidates claiming preference based on contract-based work experience

  • (v) Category Certificates: Caste Certificate, Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate, or EWS (Economically Weaker Section) Certificate as applicable.

  • (vi) Residential Proof: Permanent Residence / Domicile Certificate.

  • (vii) Freedom Fighter Ward Certificate: For grandsons/granddaughters of freedom fighters of Bihar State.

  • (viii) Disability Certificate: For candidates claiming reservation under the PwD category.

Crucial Compliance Notes

  • Upload Requirement: Failure to upload any required certificate or mark sheet may result in the cancellation of your candidacy.

  • Consistency: Only certificates mentioned in the original online application will be considered valid.

  • Deadline for Documents: All uploaded certificates and mark sheets must be issued on or before October 23, 2026.
